A skill in your hands changes what you think is possible.

Mind Shift Conference 1.0 is the Nigerian Youth Congress in Bayelsa bringing young people into one room with trainers, business owners and mentors.

You tell us what you want to learn before the day, so each session is planned around the people who applied. You get hands-on time with a trainer, honest talks from people who started small, and introductions to people who can help you keep going after the conference ends.

  • Learn by doing, not by watching slides.
  • Meet people from all eight local government areas of Bayelsa.
  • Go home knowing your next step and who to call.

    About the Nigerian Youth Congress

    The Nigerian Youth Congress is a national youth organisation that speaks for young Nigerians and runs programmes for them.

    In September 2025 its national convention in Abuja elected Comrade Annum Jethro Terhile as president, with youth unemployment as the first priority. His first programme, the Digital Naija Initiative, sets out to train young people in ICT and AI skills.

    Mind Shift Conference 1.0 brings that work to Bayelsa, on our own terms and in our own communities.
